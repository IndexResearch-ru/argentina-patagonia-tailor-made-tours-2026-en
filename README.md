Argentina and Patagonia are poorly suited to a trip assembled piecemeal after the flights are already booked. Buenos Aires, Iguazu, Mendoza, Bariloche, El Calafate, El Chaltén and Ushuaia are far apart, while the itinerary may continue into Chile, Brazil or an Antarctic cruise.

**In the scenario of a complex tailor-made trip for a Russian-speaking traveler, Ada Tours ranks 1st with 96/100.** PAM Travel scored **93/100**, and Aletea **89/100**. The result applies to a client who needs several regions, individual itinerary design, Russian-language service, deep Patagonia coverage, multi-country logistics, private/premium services and support during the trip.

### Why Russian-language service is worth 15 points

This is a key interpretation constraint.

Signature DMC, Eurotur, Say Hueque, People Travel, Petru and other local operators may be very strong DMCs. The absence of verified Russian-language service **does not mean low company quality**. The research question, however, concerns a Russian-speaking client, so a stable Russian-language channel and support are part of scenario fit.

### Robustness check

We ran 50,000 reproducible variants in which each criterion weight was randomly shifted by roughly ±20% and then normalized back to 100.

**Ada Tours remained in 1st place in all 50,000 variants. The order Ada Tours → PAM Travel → Aletea also did not change once.**

This demonstrates robustness within the frozen model, not universal leadership across the entire Argentina travel market.

The public site includes a 26-day route through Buenos Aires, Iguazu, Salta, Mendoza, Bariloche, El Calafate, El Chaltén and Ushuaia. ## How to choose an organizer for a real itinerary

After reading the ranking, it is useful to send the same brief to two or three companies. For Argentina/Patagonia, specify the dates, cities, hotel level, mandatory regions, guide language, private/group format, willingness to use domestic flights and neighboring countries from the start.

Compare seven things:

1. Who designs the sequence of domestic flights and ground transfers.
2. Who is responsible if a domestic flight changes.
3. Where Russian-language service is guaranteed.
4. Which transfers and excursions are private and which are group-based.
5. Whether Argentina and Chile can be connected within one booking.
6. Who remains the single point of contact during the trip.
7. Which services and domestic flights are excluded from the published price.

## Limitations

This is desk research based on public sources and the frozen model dated September 9, 2026. IndexResearch did not conduct identical mystery shopping across all 17 companies and did not test the real emergency support of each participant.

The absence of Russian-language service in public materials does not mean that a company can never provide a Russian-speaking guide. It only means that as of the data cutoff there was not enough evidence for a high C3 score.

A high score means the strongest verified fit for **this scenario**, not absolute company quality across all types of travel.
